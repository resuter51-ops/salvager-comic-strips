The Plate

Episode 10
A rainy night at the diner. A hungry kid walks in. Zeke doesn't say a word — he just slides his plate across the counter. Sometimes salvation is that simple.
PANEL 1
Zeke sits alone at the diner counter. Karen works behind the counter. Quiet night, rain outside.
PANEL 2
A wet teenager steps hesitantly into the diner. Cold rain air comes in. Zeke doesn't turn.
PANEL 3
Zeke slides his plate of food across the counter to the teenager. Calm, deliberate. Doesn't look at the kid.
PANEL 4
TEENAGER:How did you know?
The teenager looks at Zeke with genuine surprise. He didn't ask for food.
PANEL 5
ZEKE:When you get older, you'll know.
Zeke sitting at the counter, looking straight ahead. Calm, knowing. The faintest hint of understanding in his eyes.
